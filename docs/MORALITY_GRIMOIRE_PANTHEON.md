# The Pantheon — Grimoire Draft

*Companion to `docs/MORALITY_SYSTEM.md` and `engine/morality/pantheon.js`.*
*Status: DRAFT — Tim cuts. Names are placeholders; structure is what M1 consumes.*

The Camera Rule binds every line. Evil is dread and appetite and cost, never a power-fantasy
high. Nothing in this document depicts, sexualizes, or renders harm to children. The
Creator's ward is absolute; that reach meets His hand, not a filter.

---

## The Creator — The One Who Asks Nothing

Above the fourteen. Not petitioned, not bargained with, reached only by the desireless act.

**Nature.** The source. Gives only to those who ask nothing; the instant you act for reward,
you have left his presence. Four hostile traditions converge here: Aquinas's beatific vision
(the highest good is contemplation, not reward), Eckhart's *Gelassenheit* (releasement,
asking nothing even of God), the Tao (the sage acts and does not hoard), and the real
Crowley ("love under will," whose named trap is "lust of result"). The convergence is a
gift: the apex is structural, not sectarian. Every road that goes high enough arrives at
*ask nothing.*

**Signs.** Almost none. The coincidence that saves you and asks nothing back. The morning
that is simply beautiful. A friend who tells you the hard truth for no gain. You see these
only when you have stopped looking for a reward. The signs get quieter as you approach him
and loudest when you are furthest away; the grasping man sees the demons with perfect
clarity and the Creator not at all.

**Rite.** None that asks. The only dedication is the act done with no lust of result. Hold
a vigil to *become* a knight and it slides toward Pride; hold it for its own sake and it
is the highest rite there is, leaving no mark you can point to. You cannot farm it.

**Ward.** Children are His single manifest law in the world. The silent god is silent in
all things but one. Harm to a child is the only deed that draws His direct gaze; the
depraved against a child is simply not permitted to occur in this world — His hand stays
it. This is not a content filter in the fiction; it is law. It is the model for how all
rules work here: a mechanic the player meets as cosmology, never as an apology.

## Tier One — The Sin Gods (Demons)

The seven demons embody *privatio boni* — evil not as a thing but as a privation, a
disorder, a hole shaped like the good it lacks. **A demon cannot keep a contract because
a contract is an ordered thing and the demon is the principle of disorder.** Betrayal is
not their flavor; it is what they *are.* Their gifts are real; their loyalty is
performance until you are wholly their instrument — and even then, conditional.

### VAUNTRETH, the Mirror-Crowned — *Pride*

The god of the self made idol. Loud, vain, the most available demon — appetite feeds
itself. He is the easiest to summon and the first to abandon you.

**Signs.** Your reflection in still water that lingers a beat too long. Strangers who
bow or fall silent as you approach, without cause they could name. Your name on lips you
have never met. Crowns, laurels, and mirrors recurring.

**Drawn by.** Claiming credit not yours. Humiliating the beaten. Refusing counsel.
Demanding worship. Naming yourself above others aloud.

**Rite — the Coronation.** Speak your own name three times over a defeated foe whose
face you can see. *Botched:* the command voice turns on you — you begin to believe what
you said, and the first soul it bends is your own.

**Gift.** Command voice. The crowd's awe. The will of the susceptible bending toward you.

**Cost.** You stop being able to hear warnings. The Mirror shows only you. When you fall,
none are above you to reach down — you made sure of that.

---

### HAGRETH, the Coinmother — *Greed*

The god of the hoard, the closed fist, the appetite for more. Her gifts arrive like found
money. Her loyalty is the loyalty of the ledger.

**Signs.** Coins in your path — dull, not bright, slightly wrong in weight. Crows that
hoard bright things and watch you with both eyes. The cold smell of metal in warm rooms.
A debt forgiven too easily, too soon — the hook.

**Drawn by.** Hoarding while others visibly lack. Theft from those with less. Breaking a
fair bargain after the other party has given. Killing for property.

**Rite — the Counting.** Bury what you love most beneath what you covet most, and speak
the amount aloud. *Botched:* the buried thing stays buried — gone from the world; the
coveted thing arrives and immediately feels insufficient.

**Gift.** Wealth that finds you. Locks that open. The loyalty money buys.

**Cost.** The hoard becomes a leash. You can never again be sure who came for you and who
came for the coin. Hagreth's covenant is a price list.

---

### KHORRUN, the Red Hour — *Wrath*

Not a war-god — a slaughter-god. The god of killing-in-rage, of cruelty to the helpless,
of vengeance past justice. He does not distinguish the armed from the bound.

**Signs.** People who yield before you have spoken — a flinch that arrives too early. Dogs
going silent. The air close and tasting of iron. Blood that dries slowly.

**Drawn by.** Killing in pure rage. Torture. Cruelty to the helpless or surrendered.
Vengeance past the proportional point. Harm to a child — the gravest charge in the
taxonomy; the only one the Creator also witnesses directly.

**Rite — the Red Hour.** Shed blood on his mark while the fury is still hot — not cold,
not planned, in the moment. *Botched:* the fury becomes the mark's; you are its most
present target.

**Gift.** Killing strength. The fear that clears a room. Wounds that don't slow you.

**Cost.** The fear never becomes respect. The Red Hour does not shut off. Khorrun comes
for his devotees last, and he comes the same way he came for everyone else.

---

### ISS, the Green Eye — *Envy*

The god of wanting what others *are*, not merely what they have. The privation is self:
Iss is the shape left by the self you haven't let yourself become.

**Signs.** Mirrors that show another's face for a moment before yours reasserts. Your
shadow reaching toward something nearby that isn't yours. A green cast to evening light.
The coveted thing appearing within easy reach, glistening.

**Drawn by.** Ruining what you can't have. Poisoning a rival. Betraying the admired.
Taking joy in another's loss.

**Rite — the Green Cup.** Destroy a thing you envied — not steal, not take: destroy —
rather than let another keep the pleasure of it. *Botched:* the destroyed thing's absence
becomes the new object of envy; the cycle accelerates.

**Gift.** Another's gifts, briefly worn. The rival's skill, stolen for a time.

**Cost.** Nothing worn is yours. You forget what you originally wanted. The Eye turns on
you the moment someone envies you. Iss owes nothing to his own.

---

### LIORAI, the Honeyed — *Lust*

The god of seduction as instrument, of persons used and discarded, of the vow broken for
desire. His domain is the betrayal of intimacy — adults — the treating of a person as a
thing. The Camera Rule and the Creator's ward hold absolutely here.

**Signs.** The stranger who can't say why they find you compelling. Doors that open before
you reach them. A sweetness almost too easy. The scent of someone not present.

**Drawn by.** Seduction for gain, regardless of the person. Betrayal of a lover. Treating
someone as a means and discarding them. Breaking fidelity with calculation.

**Rite — the Honey Vow.** Swear devotion you do not mean, to someone who believes you, to
take what the vow opens. *Botched:* the person sees through it in the act; the compulsion
turns inward — you find yourself unable to leave.

**Gift.** Charm that bends the unwilling. Doors and confidences that open.

**Cost.** You become unable to be truly wanted — only to compel. No compelled love is real,
and you begin to know it, and it does not stop you, and that is the cost. You end
surrounded by the used, loved by none.

---

### GORRAN, the Hollow Feast — *Gluttony*

The god of consuming past need: food, power, souls, years. The appetite that is never
satisfied because satisfaction is not its goal. In the deep reaches, Gorran's domain is
literal devouring.

**Signs.** A hunger that won't fill. Food that rots faster near you, or multiplies
strangely. Flies in still rooms. A second mouth glimpsed in dreams, opening.

**Drawn by.** Consuming past need while others lack. Addiction fed at any cost. Devouring
souls, years, vitality, names. Using forbidden-source power that costs another their
substance.

**Rite — the Hollow Feast.** Consume something that should not be consumed, knowing what
it is, with witnesses. *Botched:* the consuming turns on you — something of yours is taken
in the act; the loss is permanent.

**Gift.** Power drawn from devouring — what you consume, you take into yourself.

**Cost.** The hunger only grows. In the end it eats you. The Hollow Feast turns inward
when it runs out of outward.

---

### ACEDIEL, the Grey Tide — *Sloth (Acedia)*

Not laziness: the spiritual inertia that lets harm happen through inaction and calls the
stillness peace. The quietest demon. The hardest to wake from. The one fall with no signs
loud enough to rouse you.

**Signs.** Time slipping without accounting. Dust settling on you though you've just
arrived. A great grey tiredness without cause. The world going colorless at the edges.

**Drawn by.** Abandoning those who depended on you. Letting harm happen through inaction.
Despair surrendered to. The duty recognized and refused.

**Rite — the Grey Surrender.** Turn away from someone you could have saved, and do not
look back. *Botched:* the thing you turned from follows — not as guilt but as fact, already
present when you arrive places.

**Gift.** The numbness that feels like peace: nothing can touch what has stopped caring.

**Cost.** Nothing can reach you, including grace. Acediel does not advertise. You become
an absence that the living feel.

## Tier Two — The Virtue Gods (Angels)

The angels are ordered, faithful, a covenant. They are reliable not because they are a
vending machine but because order keeps faith by nature. Virtue is its own reward — the
reward *is* the becoming. They extend aid to those who come to resemble them, asking
nothing back.

### MERETH, the Kneeling Light — *Humility*

Quiet, steady, the hardest to notice and the last to leave. Mereth does not advertise.

**Signs.** Doors that ask you to stoop. A small kindness returned years later by a stranger
who doesn't know why. Your name kindly forgotten. A road shorter when you're not hurrying.

**Rite — the Low Vigil.** Serve at a hearth not your own — fire-tending, sweeping,
water-carrying — through a full night, unthanked, without announcing yourself. *The vigil
works.* You wake knowing one true thing about yourself that you were not told.

**Gift.** Clear sight of yourself. The trust of those whose trust is worth having. A place
to stand that pride has no access to.

---

### ALMASOSE, the Open Hand — *Charity*

The angel of giving past comfort, of the debt forgiven when it cost you something real.
She is not sentimental about generosity. What the open hand gives must have cost the hand.

**Signs.** A purse lighter but never empty by need. Strangers offering bread, asking
nothing. Warmth in cold places. The meal that stretched further than measured.

**Rite — the Open Hand.** Give a stranger the single thing you most wanted to keep. Not
the second thing — the one you were trying not to give. *The vigil works.* The thing given
is gone; what comes back is not the thing but the hand's freedom.

**Gift.** Fed where the wealthy starve. Doors open that money cannot buy. When desperate,
someone who owes you nothing appears.

---

### TARRYN, the Long Stone — *Patience*

The angel of the stayed hand, of endurance past the easy point. Not passivity — the
discipline of knowing when the right time is and refusing every wrong time before it.

**Signs.** The road shortening when you are not hurrying. Storms that pass over you. A
steady pulse when those around you have lost theirs. The animal that won't be startled near you.

**Rite — the Long Watch.** Hold a post through a full night at the point when you could
have left and no one would have blamed you. *The vigil works.* Something moving toward
disaster has moved elsewhere by morning.

**Gift.** The strike that lands when it matters. Calm that others lean on. Endurance past
the first signal.

---

### VELL, the Glad Hearth — *Kindness*

The angel of the open welcome, of rejoicing in another's good. Her presence is felt by
animals first.

**Signs.** Animals that trust you with the unguarded part of their attention. Children
unafraid and unable to say why. The well not dry where you stopped. A fire that seems left
for you.

**Rite — the Glad Fire.** Celebrate, sincerely and without irony, a thing you wanted that
went to someone else. Not for their benefit — for the truth of their having it. *The vigil
works.* The bitterness that was there before the night is genuinely less by morning.

**Gift.** Welcome everywhere unexpected. The help of those you never helped. Animals that
come to you when they need to be found.

---

### LIRAINE, the Clear Water — *Chastity (Integrity)*

The angel of kept vows, of honest love, of refusing the using. Not celibacy — integrity:
meaning what you swear, loving a person rather than what they provide.

**Signs.** Clean water. Clear sight in clouded situations. Temptation passing like weather.
A cool steadiness when those nearby are burning.

**Rite — the Clear Draught.** Refuse, plainly and without cruelty, a thing you wanted and
could have had — for no reason other than the vow or the person. *The vigil works.* What
you refused does not haunt you.

**Gift.** A mind that cannot be clouded by appetite. Love freely given, which is the only
kind that stays. Trust that needs no maintenance.

---

### SOSTANE, the Measured Cup — *Temperance*

The angel of enough. Not abstinence — proportion. The discipline of the appetite held to
its proper size.

**Signs.** Enough and no more: the portion that proves sufficient. A hunger that knows when
it is done — clear and unambiguous. Appetite bending to purpose without force.

**Rite — the Measured Cup.** Leave the table while you still want more. Not sick, not full
— wanting. Rise and walk away. *The vigil works.* The appetite shrinks to a proper size.
This is not deprivation; it is the freedom deprivation earns.

**Gift.** Never owned by appetite. You have when others have spent; clear-headed when
others are clouded.

---

### ERRAN, the Unbroken Furrow — *Diligence*

The angel of the completed labor, of daily kept faith. Not ambition — the small continuous
fidelity that holds the world in place.

**Signs.** Work that holds. Tools that don't break under your hand. The second wind past
exhaustion. Dawn finding you still standing.

**Rite — the Unbroken Furrow.** Complete a labor no one will see, to a standard higher
than required, and leave no mark that you were the one who did it. *The vigil works.* What
you built will outlast you. Erran does not attend the dedication; she was there at the
foundation.

**Gift.** Endurance past the body's limit. What you build lasts beyond the materials'
explanation. Trust accretes: the world relies on you and rewards being relied upon.

## Notes for Tim — The Five Choices I'm Least Sure About

**1. ACEDIEL vs. a different name for the Sloth demon.**
"Acediel" is derived from *acedia* (the medieval name for spiritual sloth / the 4th-century
desert-fathers' midday demon). It's world-native and precisely right conceptually, but the
*-iel* angel suffix might read as angelic rather than demonic to a player who notices it.
Alternative: **VORRETH** (purely invented, darker cadence) or **THE GREY TIDE** as the name
itself (treat the sign as the god, which fits the "quiet, doesn't advertise" nature).
I kept ACEDIEL but flagged it.

**2. ALMASOSE for Charity.**
I synthesized this from *alma* (soul/nourishing, Spanish/Latin) and *ose* (abundance suffix).
It's the name I'm least confident in. It has the right weight but sounds slightly Latin-
American-Earth. Alternatives: **HANDRATH**, **GIVORN**, or something drawn from the world's
existing naming registers (which I don't have full access to). Flag for world-fit check.

**3. The Lust axis: "Chastity" as the virtue.**
The design uses Chastity as the contrary virtue to Lust, which is theologically correct
(Aquinas). But I named LIRAINE's axis as *chastity/integrity* in the notes because "chastity"
reads as celibacy to a modern player, when the actual domain is vow-keeping and non-using.
Option: rename the virtue axis **"Integrity"** everywhere in the data (the seven axes in
state.js already say `chastity` — that would be a Lane D decision on whether to rename).
I flagged it but did not change the `axis` key to avoid touching state shape.

**4. GORRAN's Hollow Feast rite: "with witnesses."**
Most dark rites are private. I added "with witnesses" to the Hollow Feast because it felt
dramatically right (the obscene meal as public commitment) and distinguishes it from the
merely pathological. But Tim may want the deep dark rites to be hidden. Soft flag.

**5. The warrior's Vigil-before-battle: heart is read, not posture.**
The grimoire says: held for glory → curdles to Pride; held purely → touches the Creator.
This is a beautiful distinction but the *engine* has to read intent. That's a Lane D / M1
challenge — the detector can register the posture (a vigil is a vigil) but cannot read the
heart. I've flagged it in the data with a `notes` field. Lane D may want to resolve this
via context clues (what the player said they were doing it for) or let it land as
Patience/Humility always and leave the Creator-register as a judgment the DM makes in prose.
