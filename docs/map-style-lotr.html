Google Earth × Lord of the Rings — 10 directions on one region

The two poles: GOOGLE EARTH = satellite relief, true-color terrain, atmospheric haze, oblique tilt, ocean depth, continuous zoom. LORD OF THE RINGS (Baynes / C. Tolkien) = fine pen hatching, inverted-V mountain ranges, tree-cluster forests, hair-line rivers, calligraphic italic names, parchment, cartouche, restrained sepia. The interesting cross: LOTR is hand-drawn *symbol*; Google is *data* — so the strongest directions render real terrain data THROUGH the ink hand (hatch density = slope, gold = ridgeline, contour = elevation). Eight push to the edges; two stay faithful as anchors.
