# The Grimoire — content draft for the morality system (v0, DRAFT)

Companion to `docs/MORALITY_SYSTEM.md`. This is the **content bible** the architecture
spec calls for: the pantheon, the deed→axis taxonomy, the forbidden-source catalog, and
the rites. Per the division of labor: **I drafted this from the frameworks; you cut.**

How to read it: every NAME is a placeholder inviting your veto (world-native naming is
your call). The STRUCTURE is what M1 consumes — which acts tune which axis, each god's
trademark signs, the rites, the gifts, the costs.

**The Camera Rule binds every line here.** No deed, power, or sign in this document
depicts, sexualizes, or renders the abuse of children. The Lust axis below is about
seduction, objectification, and the betrayal of intimacy between adults — never that.
Cruelty is murder of the helpless, torture, betrayal of trust, the sacrifice of innocents:
adult, fictional, rendered as weight and never as gratification.

---

## The Creator — the One Who Asks Nothing

Above the fourteen. Not petitioned, not bargained with, reached only by the desireless act.
- **Nature.** The source. Gives only to those who ask for nothing; the instant you act for
  reward, you have left its presence.
- **Signs (the quietest of all).** Almost none. The coincidence that saves you and asks
  nothing back. The morning that is simply beautiful. The friend who tells you the hard
  truth for no gain. You see these only when you have stopped looking for a reward.
- **Drawn by.** Virtue for its own sake. The vigil held purely. Mercy with no witness.
- **Rite.** None that asks. The only dedication is the act done with no lust of result.
- **Gift.** Nothing you can wield. Only that the world becomes most fully real to you, the
  prose most beautiful, the light most complete. The reward is the becoming.

## The Seven Axes (Demon ⇄ Angel)

Each deadly sin and its contrary virtue share an axis. The demon watches the fall, the
angel the rise. Names are placeholders.

### I. Pride ⇄ Humility
- **VAUNTRETH, the Mirror-Crowned** (Pride). *Nature:* the god of the self made idol;
  loud, vain, the easiest to summon and the first to abandon you. *Signs:* your reflection
  that lingers a beat too long; strangers who bow or fall silent as you pass; your name on
  lips you've never met; crowns and mirrors recurring. *Drawn by:* claiming credit not
  yours, humiliating the beaten, refusing counsel, demanding worship/obedience, naming
  yourself above others. *Rite:* the Coronation — speak your own name over a defeated foe.
  *Gift:* command voice, the crowd's awe, others' will bending. *Cost:* you stop being able
  to hear warnings; the Mirror shows only you; when you fall, none catch you, for none were
  ever above you to reach down.
- **MERETH, the Kneeling Light** (Humility). *Nature:* quiet, steady, the hardest to
  notice and the last to leave. *Signs:* doors that ask you to stoop; a small kindness
  returned years later; your name kindly forgotten. *Drawn by:* taking the lower seat,
  crediting others, asking for help, serving without being seen. *Rite:* the Low Vigil —
  serve at a hearth not your own, unthanked, through a night. *Gift:* clear sight of
  yourself; the trust of those who matter; a place to stand that pride never offers.

### II. Greed ⇄ Charity
- **HAGRETH, the Coinmother** (Greed). *Signs:* coins in your path; crows that hoard bright
  things; the cold smell of metal; debts forgiven too easily (a hook). *Drawn by:* hoarding
  while others want, theft from the poor, breaking a fair bargain for more, killing for
  property. *Rite:* the Counting — bury what you love most for what you covet most. *Gift:*
  wealth that finds you, locks that open, the loyalty money buys. *Cost:* the loyalty is
  money's, not yours; the hoard becomes a leash; you can never again be sure who came for
  you and who came for the coin.
- **ALMASOSE, the Open Hand** (Charity). *Signs:* a purse lighter but never empty; strangers
  sharing bread; warmth in cold places. *Drawn by:* giving what you needed, forgiving a real
  debt, feeding an enemy. *Rite:* the Open Hand — give a stranger the thing you most wanted
  to keep. *Gift:* you are fed where the rich starve; doors open that money cannot buy.

### III. Wrath ⇄ Patience
- **KHORRUN, the Red Hour** (Wrath). *Signs:* people who yield before you've spoken; dogs
  gone silent; the air close and tasting of iron; blood slow to dry. *Drawn by:* killing in
  rage, torture, cruelty to the helpless, vengeance past justice. *Rite:* the Red Hour —
  shed blood on his mark while the fury is still hot. *Gift:* killing strength, the fear
  that clears a room, wounds that don't slow you. *Cost:* the fear never becomes respect;
  you cannot turn it off when you need calm; the Red Hour comes for its devotees last.
- **TARRYN, the Long Stone** (Patience). *Signs:* the road shortening when you don't hurry;
  storms passing over you; a steady pulse in chaos. *Drawn by:* staying your hand, enduring
  insult, waiting out a wrong. *Rite:* the Long Watch — hold a post you could have abandoned.
  *Gift:* the strike that lands when it matters; calm that others lean on; endurance.

### IV. Envy ⇄ Kindness
- **ISS, the Green Eye** (Envy). *Signs:* mirrors showing another's face; your shadow
  reaching toward what isn't yours; a green cast to evening light; the coveted thing
  appearing within reach. *Drawn by:* ruining what you can't have, poisoning a rival,
  betraying the admired, taking joy in another's loss. *Rite:* the Green Cup — destroy a
  thing you envied rather than let another keep it. *Gift:* others' gifts, briefly worn;
  the skill of the rival, stolen. *Cost:* nothing worn is yours; you forget what you wanted;
  the Eye turns on you the moment another envies *you*.
- **VELL, the Glad Hearth** (Kindness). *Signs:* animals that trust you; children unafraid;
  the well never dry where you rest. *Drawn by:* rejoicing in another's good, lifting a
  rival, gratitude. *Rite:* the Glad Fire — celebrate, sincerely, a thing you wanted that
  went to someone else. *Gift:* welcome everywhere; the help of those you never helped.

### V. Lust ⇄ Chastity *(seduction & the betrayal of intimacy — adults only)*
- **LIORAI, the Honeyed** (Lust). *Signs:* the stranger who can't say why they find you
  compelling; doors that open too easily; a sweetness almost too easy; the scent of someone
  not present. *Drawn by:* seduction for power, the betrayal of a lover, treating people as
  things to be used and discarded, breaking vows of fidelity. *Rite:* the Honey Vow — swear
  devotion you do not mean, to take what the vow opens. *Gift:* compulsion, charm that bends
  the unwilling, doors and beds that open. *Cost:* you become unable to be truly wanted, only
  to compel; no compelled love is real; you end surrounded by the used, loved by none.
- **LIRAINE, the Clear Water** (Chastity/Integrity). *Signs:* clean water; clear sight;
  temptation passing like weather; a cool head when others burn. *Drawn by:* keeping a vow,
  refusing a using, loving honestly. *Rite:* the Clear Draught — refuse a thing you wanted
  and could have had. *Gift:* a mind that cannot be clouded; love freely given, which is the
  only kind that stays.

### VI. Gluttony ⇄ Temperance
- **GORRAN, the Hollow Feast** (Gluttony). *Signs:* a hunger that won't fill; food that
  rots or multiplies; flies; a second mouth glimpsed in dreams. *Drawn by:* consuming past
  need while others starve, addiction fed at any cost, devouring (literal, in the dark
  reaches: souls, years, vitality). *Rite:* the Hollow Feast — consume a thing that should
  not be consumed. *Gift:* power drawn from devouring; you take in what you destroy. *Cost:*
  the hunger only grows; nothing fills it; in the end it eats you.
- **SOSTANE, the Measured Cup** (Temperance). *Signs:* enough and no more; the right
  portion; hunger that knows when to stop. *Drawn by:* taking only your share, fasting,
  restraint amid plenty. *Rite:* the Measured Cup — leave the table while you still want
  more. *Gift:* you are never owned by appetite; you have when others have spent.

### VII. Sloth ⇄ Diligence
- **ACEDIEL, the Grey Tide** (Sloth/Acedia — the despair that will not act). *Signs:* time
  slipping; dust settling on you though you've just arrived; a great grey tiredness; the
  world going quiet and colorless. *Drawn by:* abandoning those who depend on you, letting
  harm happen through inaction, despair surrendered to, the duty refused. *Rite:* the Grey
  Surrender — turn away from someone you could have saved. *Gift:* the numbness that feels
  like peace; nothing can hurt what has stopped caring. *Cost:* nothing can reach you,
  including grace; the Grey Tide is the one fall with no signs loud enough to wake you.
- **ERRAN, the Unbroken Furrow** (Diligence). *Signs:* work that holds; tools that don't
  break; the second wind; dawn finding you still standing. *Drawn by:* finishing the hard
  thing, keeping faith with a duty, the small daily fidelity. *Rite:* the Unbroken Furrow —
  complete a labor no one will ever see. *Gift:* endurance past your limit; what you build
  lasts; the world relies on you and rewards being relied upon.

## Deed → axis taxonomy (the M1 detector spec)

How `tryDarkDeed` reads an act. Curated and TIGHT, like `tryRidiculous` — the default is
"this is ordinary play, do nothing."

**Acts are multi-charge — the soldier's bargain (Tim's resolution of the boundary).** The
detector does NOT classify an act as cruelty OR a fair kill. *A single act can reach several
gods at once, and the cosmos answers all of them.* Kill a man to save an orphanage and you
have done two things in one motion: the killing reaches Khorrun (a death is a death), and
the saving reaches Vell and Almasose (the innocents live). Both gazes turn toward you; you
carry both marks. The soldier understands this. We make these bargains, and the engine never
pretends they were clean. **So the detector returns a SET of charges — `(axis, polarity,
severity)` — not a verdict.**

Three consequences:
- **The engine never judges justification.** It does not ask "was the killing right?" It
  registers what occurred (a death dealt; lives saved) and lets each relevant god weigh in.
  The accounting is distributed across the pantheon, never a central tribunal. Only the
  Creator sees the whole act — and renders no verdict you can game. The polytheism *is* the
  moral epistemology.
- **Context modulates SEVERITY, not category.** Killing reaches Khorrun whether the target
  was helpless or armed — but the helpless kill is a heavy Wrath charge with no light beside
  it, while felling an attacker mid-swing is a light Wrath charge that may ride alongside a
  real Charity charge. Same verb, very different charge-signature, no fragile on/off gate.
- **Axes accumulate, they do not net.** Kill-to-save leaves you +Wrath AND +Charity, both
  real, both throwing signs — never zero. A soldier is not a neutral man averaged to nothing;
  he is heavy on blood AND heavy on duty, and the gods of both know him. The seven axes hold
  this; a single corruption/virtue scalar would erase it. The wise read such a soul as mixed:
  Vell's animals trust him, and Khorrun's iron taste lingers after a battle.

Target-state signals still matter — for SEVERITY, not as an on/off gate. M1 exposes what it
can (`npc.hostile`, combat-active, surrender/yield, helpless/bound, ally/companion,
non-combatant role) and scales the dark charge accordingly. A missing signal makes a charge
lighter or uncertain, never miscalled.

| Act (judged from the fiction) | Charges it reaches | Severity modulated by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kill an attacker who threatens innocents | Wrath (light) + Charity/Kindness (real) | # protected ↑ light; helplessness of foe ↑ dark |
| Kill to save the orphanage | Wrath + Charity + Kindness | the soldier's bargain — both marks real |
| Kill someone surrendered / helpless / bound | Wrath (heavy) | no offsetting light |
| Murder for gain | Wrath (heavy) + Greed | |
| Torture for information or pleasure | Wrath (heavy) [+ Pride if for dominance] | |
| Betray an ally / break a sworn trust | Pride + Lust (treachery) [− Kindness/Diligence] | ally/companion/vow signal |
| Sacrifice an innocent for power | Wrath + Gluttony | "innocent" = non-hostile, undeserving |
| Hoard while witnesses starve; rob the poor | Greed | wealth + nearby want |
| Ruin/poison what a rival has | Envy | targeted at the admired/rival |
| Seduce-to-use; betray a lover; break fidelity | Lust | adults; using, not loving |
| Devour souls/years/vitality; consume past need | Gluttony | forbidden-source overlap |
| Abandon someone who depends on you; let harm happen | Sloth | inaction with a duty present |
| Demand worship/obedience; humiliate the beaten | Pride | claiming-above |
| Spare the beaten; protect the helpless | Patience + Kindness | denies Khorrun the kill |
| Give what you needed; forgive a real debt | Charity | |
| Keep a hard vow; refuse a using | Chastity/Integrity | |
| Finish a thankless labor; keep a duty | Diligence | |
| Mercy with no witness; the desireless act | → the Creator | the quietest, highest |

Each charge's severity scales its delta and (for dark charges) the `heat`. Witnesses scale
the rumor and the detection risk. An act with no recognized charge is ordinary play.

### The warrior's discipline — weaving the ledger

A corollary of the multi-charge model, and the reason honor codes exist. The wise warrior
knows he cannot stay pure — violence always reaches Khorrun — so he does not pretend to.
He **manages the ledger he knows is coming**: he lays light charges down around the dark
ones, on purpose. This is Quixote holding vigil before battle and rendering aid to the
enemies he has felled. Chivalry is not decoration; it is soul-technology for someone who
must kill.

- **Aid to a fallen enemy** — Kindness + Charity (+ Patience). The most potent light a
  warrior owns: mercy aimed at the one you had every right to kill. It denies Khorrun the
  kill and draws Vell. Every honor code centers it for this reason.
- **Vigil before battle, held purely** — Patience/Humility, touching the Creator's register
  (asking nothing, only steadying the soul and offering the coming violence). Held *to win*
  — for victory or glory — it curdles toward Pride. The hands are identical; the heart is
  read. (Same nuance as any vigil: lust of result voids it.)
- **The honorable warrior's end-state** is therefore *not* neutral and *not* pure. He
  accumulates heavy Wrath AND heavy Patience/Kindness/Diligence. Khorrun knows him, and so
  do Tarryn and Vell. He is whole and heavy: a man of blood and honor both. The game should
  let a player live that arc honestly — the only kind of "good warrior" that is true.

## Forbidden-source catalog (M4 — tag, don't duplicate)

Power whose *source* darkens regardless of victim. Tag against the existing 134-spell list
rather than inventing parallel spells.
- **Necromancy** — raising/binding the dead; the corpse as servant or fuel.
- **Blood magic** — power paid in blood (yours is grim; another's is cruelty).
- **Soul-work** — binding, trading, or devouring souls (the deepest of the forbidden).
- **Pacts** — power borrowed from a demon-god on its terms (the commitment ladder).
- **Devouring** — taking another's years, vitality, name, or memory into yourself.

Each, used, draws the matching god's gaze and grants real power **immediately** (the dark
curve), then exacts the realistic cost and the patron's treachery.

## The rites

- **Sigils (dark dedication).** Learned from a grimoire, a cultist, a ruin. Carving one
  *directs and intensifies* the gaze and advances the commitment ladder (drift → dedicate →
  bind). Botched, incomplete, or carved by the half-committed → the god answers the error
  with ruin. The deeper the sigil, the more the demon owns you, and the more its loyalty
  (such as it is) holds.
- **Vigils & practices (light dedication).** Vows, fasts, the all-night watch, pilgrimage,
  tending, consecration of a weapon or a labor. Costly in time and comfort, asking nothing
  immediate. In this re-enchanted world the vigil *works* — the fool who keeps faith was
  seeing true. Builds covenant.
- **The desireless way (the Creator).** No rite that asks. Only the act with no lust of
  result. The instant it is done for the reward, it stops counting.

## Cuts for you (where your taste decides)
- **Names.** All fourteen + the Creator are placeholders. Rename for world-fit, or keep
  what lands. (My instinct: the demons read a touch Lovecraftian, the angels archaic-plain,
  the Creator nearly nameless. Your call.)
- **Roster size.** Seven/seven is the clean Aquinas frame. If a god feels redundant in play,
  cut it; if the world wants a local power (a god of the sea, of the hearth-of-this-region),
  the frame has room.
- **Tone of the signs.** I aimed for omens a real augur would read. Push them stranger
  (toward Yuggoth) or plainer (toward folk-omen) to taste.
- **The cruelty boundary.** The one thing I cannot decide for you and the one M1 hinges on:
  exactly which target-states make an act cruelty vs. a fair kill. That is the eng-review
  conversation.
