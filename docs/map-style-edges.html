Google Earth × Ultima — 10 directions on one region

Stolen DNA: confident black ink + honest grid (Dyson) · lit islands in a black void (battlemap) · warm sandstone & roof-red landmarks, aged-green parchment, saturated biome blocks · painted oblique relief mountains + isometric ridge-frames over top-down plans · calligraphic italics, banner cartouches, compass roses.
