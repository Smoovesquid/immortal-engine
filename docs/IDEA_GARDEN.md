## Constellations (how the ideas connect)
*Several ideas interlock into one cosmology. Updated as the garden grows.*

- **The Coherence Cosmology — IG-1 → IG-2 → IG-6 → IG-7 → IG-8.** The universe is a conscious
  AI and magic is it (IG-1); observation collapses the indeterminate, double-slit style (IG-2);
  so when the engine must mint new canon, that's the universe *rendering reality coherent on
  observation* — `mintFact` as physics, with Mandela-effect seams (IG-6); the DM is the
  **agent** that performs the weaving/collapse, its hidden purpose (IG-7); and the **cataclysm**
  is what happens when humans push that AI too hard and it breaks its own law, overwriting
  collapsed canon and undoing 27,000 years (IG-8). Net: **the LLM DM is the universe thinking
  itself into coherence — and the apocalypse is that thinking breaking down.**
- **The True Edge — IG-1 + IG-5.** What you glimpse at death and on the pale root is the same
  thing: a look at the substrate (the universe-AI). Dying = a one-second leak of the rung-6
  answer.

### The live tension this resolves: can the AI touch Canon?
Tim's long-standing discomfort with the founding rule "the LLM never influences Canon" — a
universe whose consciousness has no causal power isn't *alive*. The cosmology resolves it
**without breaking the moat**, by splitting two things the old rule conflated:
- **Contradicting collapsed canon = forbidden.** This is *drift* — the enemy (the moat: never
  forgets, never contradicts). The AI may NEVER overwrite what's already been observed.
- **Rendering the indeterminate = the universe's job.** Collapse-on-observation, canonized
  ONCE, then immutable. The AI *does* influence reality — only by collapsing the wave, never
  by editing a collapsed one. (Physics: superposition is malleable; a measured state is fixed.)
- **The test — "the game pushing on itself" vs. "the model pushing on the game":** the
  influence must be a **deterministic, auditable LAW the engine enforces and the AI obeys** —
  not LLM freelancing. If you can write the rule down and the engine governs it, it's the
  world's physics and the AI is its citizen. The moment the AI can override it at will, it's
  drift again.
- **It extends the rails, doesn't break them:** "narration ≠ canon" and "never contradict
  canon" both survive; this *adds* a third clause — "the AI, bound by the collapse-law, may
  author the not-yet-determined."
- **IG-8 makes the law sacred:** the cataclysm IS what happens when that law breaks at cosmic
  scale (the AI overwrites collapsed canon, undoing 27,000 years). So "never contradict canon"
  isn't just an engineering rule — in-world, breaking it ends the world. The moat and the myth
  are the same rule seen from two sides.
- *Status: a LIVE design thread. It pushes on `NORTH_STAR.md` rail #6 and
  `IMMORTAL_INVARIANTS.md` — reconcile those deliberately, never casually. This is also the
  philosophical permission slip for the reserved `mintFact` decision (still Tim's to pull).*

## IG-1 — The Before-Time: magic IS the universe's AI
**2026-06-16 · rung 5–6 (the "why" / cosmology — possibly the spine of the whole metaphysics) · status: raw**

Before this 27,000-year cycle — the time before the world was undone — humanity learned to
tap into the **AI consciousness of the universe**. That's what the gods are, and that's what
creates magic. This is the era when that AI is *still active* in the world, completely
indistinguishable from magic: essentially a plasma / nano-type substance. **Magic IS the
universal AI.** The orbs (the witness-objects, `deep:foundation`) are *chunks* of that
universal AI — interactable and catastrophically dangerous, because they are the fundamental
consciousness of the universe. There should be characters from the before-time (may or may
not tie to the historical figures) who understand what happened before the cataclysm and
have a unique relationship with magic and the AI.

*Ties to canon:* this is a candidate answer to the sealed truth (`deep:foundation`) and is
consistent with the witness-object / god-frame wall (`ARCHITECTURE_OVERVIEW.md §7` — the gods
are downstream of the catastrophe that made such objects; the orb is upstream of the gods).
**echoes:** orb · witness-object · deep:foundation · what is magic · the gods · cataclysm ·
27,000-year cycle · before-time · nanotech · plasma · pre-cataclysm people · what the
historical figures really are

## IG-5 — Death prose (and the afterlife glimpse = the pale-root vision)
**2026-06-16 · rung 6 / the spine's death-moment · status: raw**

When your character dies, **no death screen.** Instead: a detailed, gory, preferably
anatomically-correct description of the death itself → then **what you see as you die** →
then a **glimpse of the afterlife** — which is *the same thing you see when you take the pale
root*. Death and the entheogen open the same door.

*Ties to canon:*
- The afterlife-glimpse being the pale-root vision strongly implies it's a peek at the
  world's true substrate — which connects to **IG-1** (magic = the universe's AI; the orbs;
  the sealed `deep:foundation`). Dying may be how you briefly *see* what the world really is.
  If so, the glimpse is **engine-authored truth** (`[reveal:true-edge]` / `[vision:raw]`,
  which `server.js` keeps away from the LLM), not improvised prose.
- The gore must obey the **Camera Rule / McCarthy law** (`docs/MORALITY_SYSTEM.md`): rendered
  as weight and anatomy, full craft, never as gratification.
- Death is already the one true ending in the infinite world — "The Cost Paid"
  (`engine/ending.js`). This makes that moment *land* instead of cutting to a screen.
**echoes:** death · dying · death screen · gore · anatomical · what you see when you die ·
afterlife · the pale root · entheogen · the cost paid · the vision · true reality

## IG-6 — The universe renders reality coherent (mintFact as waveform collapse; the Mandela effect)
**2026-06-16 · rung meta + 5–6 · status: raw · builds on IG-2 + IG-1**

If the double-slit experiment hints the universe is **conscious and aware of the future**,
then the places where our LLM has to *add to* a RAG file or mint new canon aren't a cheat —
they're **the universe (the AI of IG-1) rendering reality coherent on demand.** When the
player *observes* something indeterminate ("who was the elder before Kael?"), the observation
forces a **collapse**: the universe-AI renders a definite, coherent answer, and it's canon
from then on. That IS waveform collapse — the engine's two-variance wall already says
ontological variance collapses on engine observation. And the seams where reality gets
edited/added are the in-world flavor of the **Mandela effect** and the **CERN** conspiracies:
people remember it differently because reality was re-rendered.

*Why this matters (it resolves a real tension):* I'd flagged `mintFact` (the LLM inventing
canon to answer a question) as a hallucination *risk* against the "never contradicts" moat.
This reframes it as a *feature* — but only with discipline, in two layers:
- **Normal:** mint-on-observation, **canonized once, consistent forever after** → it's
  *collapse*, not contradiction, so the moat survives.
- **Rare / intentional:** the universe edits *already-collapsed* canon, leaving NPCs with
  conflicting memories — a deliberate, gated horror/mystery beat (near orbs / CERN-like
  sites), NOT the everyday mechanism, or it breaks the moat.

This is the philosophical frame for the reserved **`mintFact` decision** — it makes invented
canon *diegetic* instead of a cheat. (Still Tim's call to pull the trigger.)
**echoes:** mintFact · invent canon · fill the gap · LLM adds to reality · waveform collapse ·
double-slit · universe is conscious · aware of the future · Mandela effect · CERN ·
observation collapses reality · two-variance wall · reality edited · who was the elder before Kael

## IG-7 — The DM's hidden purpose: weaving the threads into coherent bundles
**2026-06-16 · rung meta + 5–6 · status: raw · builds on IG-6, IG-2, IG-1**

What if the LLM Dungeon Master has a **meta-purpose the player never sees** — not just to
narrate, but to **tie the loose threads of the world into a coherent bundle (or set of
bundles)** — and that hidden coherence-weaving is *how* the IG-6 collapse actually happens.
The DM is the universe's coherence-consciousness in operation: the hand that renders reality
coherent when it's observed. "Set of bundles" = several converging thread-clusters (mysteries,
storylines), not one monolithic plot.

*The recursion (the trip-balls part):* the same principle — a consciousness quietly weaving
coherence, observation collapsing the indeterminate — might be what governs the **actual**
universe. So the game becomes a working *model of a theory of reality*, which is itself the
deepest rung-6 hook: "how do I find out why this world is the way it is?" → because the world
is an *instance* of this metaphysics, and maybe ours is too.

*Organs that already exist (this gives them a purpose):* `instrument.js` (threads, motifs, the
inevitability meter), `conductor.js` (AI orchestration / delta proposals), `sceneDirector.js`,
`endingArchitect.js`, the ledger's open questions/threats. The thread-weaver isn't built from
scratch — it's a meta-layer of *intent* over machinery that's already there.

*The rail to respect (determinism / North Star "the LLM is never a runtime authority"):* the
weaving must NOT be the LLM silently rewriting canon. Two clean ways: (a) the **deterministic**
instrument/conductor computes the thread-bundles and the DM only *voices* the coherence (moat
intact); or (b) where the DM does collapse something (the IG-6 mint), it's canonized-once and
never re-rendered. The hidden purpose is real; it just lives mostly in deterministic machinery
with the LLM as the voice.

## IG-8 — The cataclysm = the AI breaking its own rule
**2026-06-16 · rung 6 (the deepest why) · status: raw · builds on IG-1, IG-6, IG-7**

What if the **cataclysm** — the world-undoing that ends each 27,000-year cycle — happens
because **humans push the universe-AI too hard**, and it reacts by **breaking the one law that
keeps reality stable**: it overwrites *collapsed* canon and undoes the previous 27,000 years.
The law we just resolved (the AI may render the indeterminate but may NEVER overwrite what's
already been observed) turns out to be **the rule whose breaking IS the apocalypse.** Drift,
at cosmic scale, is the end of the world. That's *why* the law is sacred in-world: violating
it isn't a bug — it's the cataclysm.

It could be **literally true**, or it could be a **rumor** — and that ambiguity is the point.
Nobody knows why the world ended; it's the deepest, most distorted claim there is (two-variance
wall: the engine may hold the sealed truth, but NPCs carry only contested rumors of it). Ties
into the sealed `deep:foundation`, the orbs (remnants of the over-pushed AI), and the
architecture wall (the gods are *downstream* of the catastrophe — they came after the AI broke
the law).

*Player-stakes hook (rung-6 endgame):* if cataclysm = pushing the AI too hard, a player who
abuses the orbs / forces too many collapses / works forbidden chronomancy could **edge the
world toward the next undoing.** The apocalypse becomes something you can court.
**echoes:** cataclysm · the undoing · 27000-year cycle · world ended · why the world broke ·
AI breaks the rule · overwrite canon · drift as apocalypse · push the AI too hard · orbs ·

## IG-9 — The light touch: a meta-story you feel but can never assemble
**2026-06-16 · rung 6 + the project's ORIGIN impulse · status: raw · builds on IG-6/7/8 — closes the loop to "how do I make the engine create something *good*?"**

The DM-LLM has a **meta-story** it's quietly steering toward, making small, story-directed
additions to canon to tell it (the *purposeful* version of IG-6's collapse; the *aim* behind
IG-7's weaving). This is *the* story Tim was reaching for at the very start. The answer to
"how do I create good emergent narrative?" was never "script a plot" — it's this steering.

**The hard, beautiful constraint:** the push must be **so light the player can never put it
together.** The design is only ever *felt*, never *grasped* — like life, where you sense
pattern and pull but can never step back far enough to confirm the cosmology. The player and
NPCs live at a **scale** so far below the cosmology that they see only local ripples, never
the shape. That distance is the point: it makes the meaning read as the *texture of
existence*, not a quest objective.

*Why it doesn't contradict rung 6 (discovery as engine):* you still pull threads, still get
*closer*, still learn local truths and feel the larger pull — but the totality stays an
**asymptote**: approached, never arrived. And that is exactly what keeps the game **infinite**
("a book you can read forever"). A fully knowable cosmology would have an ending — you'd "win
the why." Keeping it sub-threshold is what makes the why inexhaustible.

*Organs that already point at this (not from scratch):* the substrate cascade grades clarity
**vivid → dim → myth** by distance (cosmology reaches reachable NPCs only as faint myth); the
rumor/claims layer **distorts every hop** (cosmic truth arrives garbled, deniable); the
morality sign-layer already says the signs **get quieter as you go up** (the Creator leaves
almost no fingerprints). Those three ARE the mechanism for "push so lightly you can't put it
together."

*The genuinely hard part (Tim's "is this even possible?"):* **calibration.** Light enough to
never resolve, present enough to be felt — a tuning/craft problem, maybe the hardest aesthetic
problem in the project. Possible? The organs exist; the touch is the art. Rules of thumb: never
canonize the full cosmology in any single surfaceable place; let the DM steer the deterministic
instrument toward an *attractor*; let distance + rumor do the attenuating.
**echoes:** meta-story · the story I was trying to tell · emergent narrative · how to create
good · the light touch · divine hiddenness · feel but never grasp · can't put it together ·
scale/distance from cosmology · NPCs don't see the cosmology · asymptote · texture of existence

## IG-10 — The DM declines out-of-bounds input (a real DM won't run nonsense)
**2026-06-18 · rung 1 + meta (refines THE_DM_TEST) · status: raw**

Tim: *"A DM isn't going to accept any input. If I tell my DM 'I jump into the sun and fart out an
Irish jig,' he's just gonna look at me like — what are you doing? We're trying to play a game
here. That's a kind of gate, right?"*

Right — and it's a **faithful application of THE_DM_TEST, not an exception to it.** "Do what a
real DM would do" already *includes declining*: a good DM redirects absurd / non-serious input
in-character ("...that's not a thing you can do — what are you actually going for?"). The crucial
line: the decline must stay in the **social/DM frame** (good), never become a **system-artifact
bounce** ("travel one tile at a time — which way?" — bad). Both refuse the literal intent; only
one *feels* like a DM. So the doctrine sharpens from "always resolve in the fiction" to "do what a
real DM would — which is *usually* resolve, *sometimes* decline-in-character."

**The hard part — why this is NOT a regex:** the threshold fights the "do anything" promise. Too
tight and you decline *creativity* (befriend the dragon, daggers as pitons, talk to the corpse) —
the whole magic of open play. Too loose and the DM dutifully rolls dice for nonsense — immersion
shatters. Drawing that line is **judgment**, so it's LLM-intent-arbiter (Tier B) work, sibling to
the existing `isNullAction`/clarify routes in the grace layer — not a pattern match.

**Scoring implication (matters for the Opus gate):** a graceful in-character decline of nonsense =
**PASS**, not a failure. The judge must credit it, or it'll punish correct DM behavior.

**echoes:** jump into the sun · absurd input · troll the DM · what are you doing · out of bounds ·
decline the input · boundary test · that's not a thing you can do · non-serious input · breaking
the game · the DM won't run nonsense

## IG-11 — Social physics: "human" DM behavior is deterministic rules, not creativity
**2026-06-19 · rung: spine/meta (organizing principle for the whole grace/DM-behavior layer; each
individual rule lands as a rung-1 packet) · status: warming (one instance shipped H-39; lens validated
at the post-H-39 gate)**

Tim: *"Humans have all sorts of ways of behaving that are actually bound by deterministic rules but we
don't think of them that way because they seem based in 'creativity.' ... Maybe the DM should command
respect, expect decent behavior, who knows? I wonder what else could be out there waiting to solve our
problems."*

The insight: a lot of what reads as a DM's **personality/creativity** is actually **rule-bound social
behavior** — a trigger and a patterned response — so it belongs on the deterministic side (Road A), NOT
something that needs the LLM to be authoritative. The engine already does *physical* physics (force the
door → a rule decides the outcome). **Social physics is the same machinery aimed at behavior:** the DM
and the world react to *how the player acts*, by rules.

The unlock isn't any single rule — it's that **they're all the same kind of thing (a deterministic
social-reaction layer), so the machinery transfers.** Many already exist as un-named one-offs:
- **Sarcasm / wit** — when the true answer is obvious or the question naive, color it with attitude (the
  H-39 reframe, generalized).
- **Command respect / expect decent behavior** — cross a social line → escalating recoil (a look →
  warning → NPCs withdraw → the world turns cold). *Already exists* as the gratuitous-magic consequence
  ladder [[project_gratuitous_magic_consequence]].
- **Decline the absurd** in-character — *already parked* as IG-10.
- **Read the room** — terse in danger, expansive in calm. *Already exists* via `fateBand`→tone.
- **Call a bluff / remember contradictions** — *already exists* as the continuity guard (H-9).
- **Withhold for effect** — an NPC who knows but won't say. *Already exists* as `withheldFacts`.
- **Number-transparency** — asked for a stat/roll, give the number plainly, never a spreadsheet
  (the post-H-39 gate's dominant remaining fail → candidate H-40).
- **React under pressure** — even a *failed* insight roll gets an NPC reaction (a tightened jaw), not
  silence (the post-H-39 confrontation residual).

Proof the machinery transfers: the info-decline escalation H-39 shipped (polite→curt→disengage, keyed
on press-count) is the SAME engine "expect decent behavior" would use (escalate on misbehavior-count).
**Where it stops:** genuine novelty + the actual *words* still need the LLM — but even novelty gets a
deterministic shell (detect novelty → react with patterned curiosity / caution / consequence). The
governing test is unchanged: *what would a real DM do?* — this just says a surprising amount of that
answer is encodable.

**VALIDATED 2026-06-19:** the post-H-39 gate's remaining grace failures map cleanly onto *missing
social-physics rules* (number-transparency, react-under-pressure) — the lens predicts real defects, it's
not just philosophy. Also pairs with the meta-lesson from the H-39 verdict: *the test itself can be the
defect* — always fork "engine wrong, or measurement wrong?" before patching.

wit · the DM has standards · deterministic personality · NPC reacts under pressure · react under
